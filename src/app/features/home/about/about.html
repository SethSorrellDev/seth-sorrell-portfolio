From the plant floor to production code

I'm a Service Support Representative at Cintas' Frankfort, Indiana plant — and I'm building my way into a software engineering seat at Cintas corporate, one shift and one shipped feature at a time.

Field service gave me something a bootcamp couldn't: I've lived the operational problems I write code for. I know what it's like when a route runs long, when an inspection report doesn't match reality, when a schedule swap falls through the cracks at 6am. That's the domain behind every app in this portfolio — a fleet inspection system with full RBAC, a VRPTW route optimizer, a workforce scheduler, and the records system tying it together, plus an identity layer so they actually trust each other instead of just pretending to be one product.

I'm finishing a CS degree through IU Online, picking up certifications along the way, and treating LeetCode like part of the job. I'm staying in my current role until the internal transfer happens — this isn't a portfolio for a job search outside Cintas, it's the case I'm building for the one inside it.
